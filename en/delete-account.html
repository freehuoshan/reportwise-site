Delete your ReportWise account and data

This page explains how to delete your account in the ReportWise app, exactly what is removed, and what (if anything) is kept.

How to delete your account in the app

The primary deletion flow is inside ReportWise:

  1. Open ReportWise.
  2. Go to Me → Privacy & Account → Delete Account.
  3. Review the deletion details and confirm.

When deletion succeeds, ReportWise deletes the server-side records tied to your account and clears local ReportWise data from that device. You do not need to email support if you can use the in-app flow.

If you cannot use the app

Email reportwise.support@codeblog.net with the subject "Delete my account" and include one of the following so we can identify your account:

  • The Google account or other account identifier you bound to ReportWise — send the request from that email address; or
  • Your anonymous Account ID, found in the app under Me → Privacy & Account or the app's account details screen.

We will delete your records and confirm by reply, typically within 30 days of the request.

What is deleted

On a verified request we permanently delete the server-side records tied to your account:

  • Your anonymous account and its device identity;
  • Any bound Google identity or other linked account identifier;
  • Your entitlement, free-credit state, AI credit balance, and purchase-grant records;
  • Your OpenAI data-sharing consent records;
  • Your AI analysis task records;
  • Any temporary encrypted AI result still associated with the account.

When you delete from inside the app, ReportWise also clears local report images, local metadata, and local analysis data from that device.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Report images and prompts are not retained on our servers. Images and prompts submitted for analysis are passed through to OpenAI to produce the analysis you requested, then discarded by ReportWise after the response is returned.
  • Analysis results are not stored long-term. They may be held temporarily in encrypted form so your device can retrieve the response, then automatically expire and are purged.
  • Reports stored on other devices stay under your control on those devices. Delete the account in the app on each device, delete individual reports, or uninstall the app to remove local data from another device.
  • Payment and refund history is handled by Apple and Google, not by ReportWise. That platform-side transaction history is governed by Apple/Google and is not something we can delete on your behalf; manage it through your Apple ID or Google Play account.
  • Data shared with OpenAI is governed by OpenAI's own privacy terms. ReportWise does not keep a copy of your uploaded images or prompts after the response is returned.
  • We may briefly retain minimal records where required to comply with legal, tax, or fraud-prevention obligations; these are deleted once those obligations lapse.

Questions

For any privacy or data request, email reportwise.support@codeblog.net. See also our Privacy Policy. Typical reply within 48 hours.
