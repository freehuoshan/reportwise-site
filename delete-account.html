Delete your ReportWise account and data

This page explains how to ask the ReportWise team to delete your account and the data tied to it, exactly what is removed, and what (if anything) is kept.

How to request deletion

Email reportwise.support@codeblog.net with the subject "Delete my account" and include one of the following so we can identify your account:

  • The Google or Apple account you bound to ReportWise — send the request from that email address; or
  • Your anonymous Account ID, found in the app under Settings → About → Account ID.

We will delete your records and confirm by reply, typically within 30 days of the request.

What is deleted

On a verified request we permanently delete the server-side records tied to your account:

  • Your anonymous account and its device identity;
  • Any bound Apple or Google identity;
  • Your entitlement / AI credit balance and purchase-grant records;
  • Your AI analysis task records.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Report images and analysis results are not retained on our servers. Images you submit are used only to produce the analysis you requested. Analysis results are end-to-end encrypted and automatically expire and are purged shortly after the analysis completes — they are never stored long-term, with or without a deletion request.
  • Reports stored on your device stay under your control: delete an individual report inside the app (its files are removed from the device), or uninstall the app to remove all local data.
  • Payment and refund history is handled by Apple and Google, not by ReportWise. That platform-side transaction history is governed by Apple/Google and is not something we can delete on your behalf; manage it through your Apple ID or Google Play account.
  • We may briefly retain minimal records where required to comply with legal, tax, or fraud-prevention obligations; these are deleted once those obligations lapse.

Questions

For any privacy or data request, email reportwise.support@codeblog.net. See also our Privacy Policy. Typical reply within 48 hours.
