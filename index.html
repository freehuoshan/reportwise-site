Why ReportWise

Built for people who want help reading their results without handing over a folder of personal medical history.

●

Local-first storage

Report images live in your phone's sandbox. You can export an encrypted .rwarchive backup any time, and delete a report to remove the files from your device.

●

You choose what to share

Before any image leaves your phone, you crop and paint over names, IDs, and anything else you'd rather not send. AI sees only the redacted version.

●

Family profiles

One device, multiple profiles — keep reports for parents and kids organised separately, with no cloud account needed.

How it works

Three steps, on your terms.

Import a report

Snap a photo, or pick from your library. ReportWise reads the EXIF date so each image is timestamped automatically.

Crop and redact

Drag black rectangles over anything personal — name, hospital ID, address. The redaction is burned into the image before anything is uploaded.

Ask the AI

Pick a date range, a model, and ask a question — "explain this overall", "what changed since last time", "what's outside the normal range?". Up to 20 reports per question.

FAQ

Where are my reports stored?

On your phone, in the app's sandbox storage. They never sync to a cloud account on their own. You control export and deletion.

Do I need internet?

You need internet only when you tap "analyze" — that's when the redacted images travel to the AI model. Importing, viewing, organising and deleting reports all work offline.

Does ReportWise's server keep my images?

No. Our proxy server forwards your request to the AI provider and discards the image as soon as the response comes back. We keep only a thin task record (model, duration, status) for billing and reliability — no image data.

Is this medical advice?

No. ReportWise is not a medical device and the AI output is not a diagnosis. Always confirm anything important with a licensed clinician.

How do I delete my data?

Delete a report inside the app to remove its files from your device. To remove the small bit of server-side data tied to your anonymous account (entitlement + AI task metadata), email reportwise.support@codeblog.net.
